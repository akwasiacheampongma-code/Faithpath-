# FaithPath Product Research Applied
- Keep FaithPath distinct from generic Bible-plan apps: the differentiator remains Bible → reflection → personal path → action → later review.
- Added guided plans because topic-based, short daily plans reduce first-use uncertainty.
- No streaks or faith scoring.
- Guided plan progress is local/offline and optional.
- Existing direct Bible, journal, path and quiz routes remain.
- Netlify deploy remains static/offline-first.
